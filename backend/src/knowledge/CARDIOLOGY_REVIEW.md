# Cardiology guidance: where it differs from the specialist guidelines

The cardiology passages were drafted by AI from NHS, WHO, CDC and NHLBI patient pages. They were then checked against the ESC and CSI guidelines cited in `guidanceSources.js`, with the verbatim evidence in `specialistEvidence.js`. They are live: the cardiology assistant answers from them.

## Fixed before going live

Two passages told patients to contact the clinic the same day in situations ESC treats as possible unstable angina, which needs emergency care:

- **`cardio-angina`**: angina that is worse, more frequent, longer or at rest, and chest pain that comes and goes.
- **`cardio-bp-emergency`**: chest pain that comes and goes.

Both now say to call an ambulance or go to the nearest hospital emergency department, in English, Bengali and Hindi.

## Still as drafted: a cardiologist should decide

A practice that disagrees with a passage can take its own copy on the Knowledge base screen, then edit it or retire it.

| Passage | The draft says | The specialist guideline says |
|---|---|---|
| `cardio-hf-self-care` | Patients may be asked to limit how much they drink. | ESC 2026 (heart failure): no evidence supports fluid restriction in chronic heart failure. Consider it only for individual patients, such as those with hyponatraemia. |
| `cardio-cholesterol-basics` | HDL "may make you less likely" to have heart problems. | ESC 2021 and CSI 2024 treat HDL as a marker of lower risk; raising it has not been shown to lower risk. |
| `cardio-statins`, `cardio-heart-medicine-side-effects` | Muscle aches are a common statin side effect. | ESC 2021: most muscle aches in people taking statins are not caused by the statin. Tell patients to report aches rather than stop the statin. |
| `cardio-cholesterol-lifestyle` | Eat less ghee, butter, cream and cheese. | CSI 2024 is more permissive about dairy fat. The draft follows ESC 2021. |
| `cardio-heart-healthy-eating` | Keep saturated fat to 10% of energy or less. | CSI 2024 says restricting it has no discernible effect in trials. ESC 2021 supports the 10% limit, and is cited. |
| `cardio-bp-numbers` | High blood pressure is 140/90 or above (135/85 at home). | Matches ESC 2024 and WHO. The US guideline uses a lower threshold, but its page could not be opened to check. |

The US ACC/AHA guidelines refused every request, so none of them is cited.
